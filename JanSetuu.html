Civic Terms Explained in Plain Language

No more confusion with difficult official paperwork vocabulary.

💳 DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)

Money sent straight from the government treasury into your linked bank account. No middlemen, no commission.

🔐 e-KYC (Electronic Know Your Customer)

Verifying your identity using your Aadhaar card and an OTP received on your mobile, or your fingerprint at a service centre.

🏦 Aadhaar NPCI Seeding

Connecting your Aadhaar number to your main bank account so national subsidies can reach your account without fail.

📜 Jamabandi / Khasra-Khatauni / Fard

Official land record papers maintained by the revenue department / Patwari that prove which piece of farmland belongs to you.
